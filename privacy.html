Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

1. What We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • National Identity Number (NIN)
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN) — if you request verification
  • Business registration details — if you request business verification

Transaction Information:

  • Listing details (item, price, description, location)
  • Escrow transaction records
  • Wallet balance and transaction history
  • Payment gateway references (Paystack/Flutterwave)
  • Chat messages with our AI assistant (for service improvement)

Device Information:

  • IP address
  • Device type (for security and fraud prevention)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide escrow services: hold funds, process transactions, release payments
  • To verify identity: NIN, BVN, and business verification for trust badges
  • To prevent fraud: AI analysis of listings and transaction patterns
  • To resolve disputes: review evidence and make fair decisions
  • To communicate: transaction updates, deal status, support messages
  • To improve our service: aggregate analytics (no individual identification)

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your transaction details with other users (except as needed for a deal)

3. Data Storage

  • Your data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database on our servers
  • Payment data is handled by Paystack/Flutterwave (PCI-DSS compliant) — we do not store card numbers
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot see your password
  • JWT tokens expire after 24 hours

4. Data Sharing

We share data only when:

  • Required by law: court order, law enforcement request, or regulatory compliance
  • For a transaction: buyer/seller see each other's names and verified badges for a specific deal
  • With payment processors: Paystack/Flutterwave receive transaction amounts
  • With logistics partners: rider dispatch service receives delivery address (for shipped deals)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of your data
  • Correct: fix inaccurate information
  • Delete: request account deletion (escrow funds must be settled first)
  • Export: download your transaction history
  • Object: opt out of AI analysis (reduced fraud protection)

To exercise these rights, email support@dealshield.ng

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data: kept as long as your account is active
  • Closed account data: retained for 5 years for fraud prevention and legal compliance
  • Transaction records: kept for 7 years (financial regulations)
  • Chat logs: kept for 1 year for dispute resolution

7. Security Measures

  • Encryption: All API traffic uses HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication: JWT tokens with 24-hour expiry
  • Rate limiting: Prevents brute force attacks
  • Security headers: HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options
  • Database security: PostgreSQL with restricted access, encrypted connections
  • Regular updates: security patches applied promptly

8. NDPA Compliance

DealShield complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. We are registered as a data controller and follow the principles of:

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
  • Purpose limitation
  • Data minimization
  • Accuracy
  • Storage limitation
  • Integrity and confidentiality

9. Cookies

DealShield uses localStorage (not cookies) for:

  • Authentication token (JWT)
  • User session (no tracking)

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

10. Children's Privacy

DealShield is not available to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users via WhatsApp/SMS/email of any material changes to this Privacy Policy.
