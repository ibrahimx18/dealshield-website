1. Introduction

DealShield ("we", "us", "our") is an escrow platform that protects buyers and sellers in transactions involving cars, gold, currency, land, oil, and other high-value goods. By using DealShield (via our website, Android app, or WhatsApp bot), you agree to these Terms.

2. How DealShield Works

  1. Buyer deposits payment into DealShield escrow
  2. Seller delivers the goods
  3. Buyer confirms receipt
  4. DealShield releases funds to the seller (minus 2.5% commission)

DealShield acts as a neutral third-party escrow agent. We do not take ownership of goods being traded.

3. Fees

  • Commission: 2.5% of the transaction amount, charged to the seller on completed deals
  • Payment Gateway Fees: Paystack/Flutterwave processing fees (1.5%) apply to deposits
  • No Hidden Fees: We do not charge for account creation, wallet storage, or withdrawals

4. User Responsibilities

Buyers must:

  • Pay the agreed amount into escrow before the seller ships
  • Confirm receipt within 48 hours of delivery, or open a dispute
  • Provide accurate delivery information

Sellers must:

  • Ship goods as described in the listing
  • Upload video proof for high-value items (gold, land)
  • Use verified logistics providers for tracked shipments

All users must:

  • Provide real name, phone number, and NIN for verification
  • Not use DealShield for illegal goods, money laundering, or fraud
  • Not create multiple accounts to manipulate reviews

5. Escrow Rules

  • Funds in escrow are held safely and cannot be accessed by DealShield for any purpose other than releasing to the seller or refunding the buyer
  • If the buyer does not confirm receipt within 7 days and does not open a dispute, funds are automatically released to the seller
  • If a dispute is opened, DealShield reviews evidence from both parties and resolves within 48 hours

6. Disputes

  • Either party may open a dispute before funds are released
  • DealShield's decision is final and binding
  • Evidence includes: chat logs, delivery confirmation, video proof, and tracking data
  • If the seller is found at fault, the buyer receives a full refund
  • If the buyer is found at fault (e.g., received goods but falsely claimed non-delivery), the seller receives the funds

7. Verification & Trust Badges

  • NIN Verified: National Identity Number confirmed
  • BVN Verified: Bank Verification Number confirmed

Trust badges are based on verified data. DealShield does not guarantee the character or reliability of any user.
