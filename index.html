How It Works

Three Steps to a Safe Deal

No complicated process. No hidden fees. Just safe, secure transactions in three simple steps.

1

Create Escrow

Buyer and seller agree on terms. Buyer deposits payment into SafePay escrow. Funds are held securely — not released until delivery is confirmed.

2

Seller Delivers

Seller ships the goods with tracking. For high-value items like gold, video proof is required. SafePay tracks every step of the logistics.

3

Money Released

Buyer confirms receipt. SafePay releases funds to the seller — minus a small 2.5% commission. Both sides leave a review. Deal complete.
