How It Works

A structured escrow process

01

Initiate Deal

Buyer or seller creates the agreement, sets inspection timeline and specifies counterparty.

02

Lock Funds

Buyer deposits funds into a dynamic bank vault or USDT escrow address. Funds are isolated instantly.

03

Fulfil & Verify

Seller delivers the asset. Buyer inspects during the verification window — cars, documents, assays.

04

Release

Funds released when conditions are satisfied. Buyer clicks release or timer auto-expires. Seller gets instant payout.
